Signs your site has been hacked

  • Visitors land on pharmacy, casino or other spam pages
  • Google shows "This site may be hacked" or "Deceptive site ahead"
  • Admin users you never created
  • Your host suspended the account for malware
  • Strange pages or links appear in search results
  • The site is suddenly slow or sends spam email

How a cleanup works

The order matters. Each step depends on the one before it.

  1. Tell me what you see

    Send the URL and what has happened. I reply with what I think is going on and what access I need.

  2. Back up and contain

    I take a full copy of the site so nothing is lost, then limit the attacker's access while I work.

  3. Find and remove

    I scan files and the database, remove malware and backdoors, and restore clean core files.

  4. Close the hole

    Outdated plugins, weak passwords and stolen credentials are fixed or replaced. Hardening is applied.

  5. Report and monitor

    You get a written summary of what was found, what was changed, and what to do to stay safe.
