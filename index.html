Five steps, one climb

Each step builds on the one before it, from your first message to a retested, safer system.

  1. Tell us what you need

    Send the website or system and what worries you. We reply with what we recommend.

  2. Agree scope and permission

    We write down what we may test, what is off limits, the dates, and how we reach each other.

  3. Test or investigate

    We scan, probe and try to break in, or work through the fault, using tools such as Nmap, Wireshark and Kali Linux.

  4. Report and fix

    You get problems ranked by risk. We apply the fixes remotely or guide your team through them.

  5. Retest and close

    We check again to confirm every problem is closed, then send you the final summary.

Permission first, always

We only test systems you own or have given us written permission to test. Before any work starts we agree what is in scope, what is off limits, and when testing happens. What we find stays between us, and we will sign an NDA if you ask.

How remote access works

  • You create a temporary account or key for us. We never ask for your main password.
  • We work in the time window we agree, and tell you what we changed.
  • When we finish you remove our access, and we give you the commands to do it.
